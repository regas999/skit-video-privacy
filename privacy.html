Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who we are

Skit Video Uploader is a private tool operated by the creator of the YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts Prompt Panik and Vibe & Bug. Contact: promptpanik@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • Only the creator's own Google accounts that own the two channels above sign in to the tool.
  • It requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information such as the channel name (youtube.readonly) to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube user.

How the data is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the creator's private server and are used only to upload the creator's videos and their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Instagram and TikTok

  • The tool also publishes the same videos to the creator's own Instagram professional accounts (@promptpanik, @vibeandbug) through the Instagram Graph API, and to the creator's own TikTok accounts through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.
  • Instagram permissions: instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management. TikTok scopes: user.info.basic (to confirm the connected account) and video.upload / video.publish (to post the creator's videos).
  • No data about other users, followers or viewers is read or stored. Access tokens are kept only on the creator's private server. Access can be removed from Facebook/Instagram settings (Apps and websites) or TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or on request to the contact address above.
